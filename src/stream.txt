Stream to laptop with ip (192.168.67.162) and port 5000

simple:

gst-launch-1.0 nvarguscamerasrc sensor_id=0 ! nvvidconv ! omxh265enc insert-vui=1 ! h265parse ! rtph265pay config-interval=1 ! udpsink host=192.168.0.107 port=5000


Reference:
gst-launch-1.0 nvarguscamerasrc sensor_id=0 ! 'video/x-raw(memory:NVMM),  width=3264, height=1848, format=(string)NV12, framerate=(fraction)28/1' ! nvvidconv flip-method=0 ! omxh265enc ! h265parse ! rtph265pay config-interval=1 !  udpsink host=192.168.0.107 port=5000
